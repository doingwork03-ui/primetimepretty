Primetime Pretty started with a sketchbook, a pot of tea, and a garden that got a little out of hand. I draw the flowers I grow (daisies, sweet peas, lavender and a few stubborn roses) and pair them with the little sayings that keep me smiling.

Each design is printed in small batches and packed by hand. I hope it brings a bit of sunshine to your morning coffee or to someone you love.
